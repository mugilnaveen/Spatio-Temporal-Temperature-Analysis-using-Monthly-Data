# Spatio-Temporal-Temperature-Analysis-using-Monthly-Data
#  Objective (Problem Statement)

* To analyze regional monthly climate data (temperature) over the period 2006–2025 in order to identify trends, seasonal patterns, and geographical variations that can support climate monitoring and decision-making.
#  Business Goal
* Understand long-term temperature trends across regions
* Identify seasonal variations and extreme months
* Support agriculture, energy planning, and climate risk management
* Enable data-driven forecasting and policy decisions
# Domain
* Domain: Climate / Environmental Data Analytics
* Sub-domain: Temperature Trend Analysis, Weather Monitoring
  #**Dataset Information**
**Source:** Open Data Portals: <font color="blue"><u> **https://power.larc.nasa.gov/data-access-viewer/**</font></u>

**Year / Timeline:** Data collected during 2006 to 2025

**Dataset Name:** POWER Regional Monthly Data (2006–2025)

**Source:** NASA POWER (Prediction Of Worldwide Energy Resources)

**Records:** 500 rows

**Columns:** 17

**Granularity:** Monthly + Annual temperature data

**Geographical Scope:** Multiple latitude (LAT) and longitude (LON) grid points
# **Tools & Technologies**
* Power BI
* Python Libraries: Pandas, Numpy
* Matplotlib, Seaborn
* Programming Language
  # **Key Insights**
- The average annual temperature is ~27.5°C, showing a generally warm climate across regions.
- The temperature range (22.9°C – 29.1°C) indicates moderate variation, not extreme but still significant.
- Year-wise trend fluctuates, with some peak years indicating possible heat spikes or climatic shifts.
- Summer months (APR–JUN) consistently record the highest temperatures across all regions.
- Winter months (NOV–JAN) show the lowest temperatures, but the drop is not drastic.
- Regions like Thanjavur and Tiruchirappalli frequently appear as top hot regions, indicating persistent heat zones.
- Some regions appear in both hot and cold categories, showing high seasonal variability.
- The heatmap highlights strong seasonal patterns, with clear color shifts from cooler to warmer months.
- Overall, regional geography plays a major role in temperature distribution.
  # **Conclusion**

The analysis clearly highlights temperature patterns across time and regions, identifies hot and cold zones, and uncovers seasonal trends. These insights provide a strong base for data-driven decision-making, especially in climate-sensitive sectors.
